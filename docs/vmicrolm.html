v-µLM (V-MicroLM) — The Dual-Engine Edge Architecture

v-µLM (V-MicroLM) is Nio's proprietary architecture for running ultra-efficient edge and resident models without relying on synthetic Q&A dataset fine-tuning. Instead of degrading reasoning through synthetic samples, v-µLM splits execution into two complementary mathematical pillars: v-µLM Reflex and v-µLM Vector.

1. The Twin Pillars

Conventional edge models often suffer from either heavy memory bloat or catastrophic forgetting caused by fine-tuning on noisy synthetic question-answer datasets. v-µLM eliminates synthetic training entirely by decoupling syntax reflexes from deep semantic reasoning:

Pillar 1: v-µLM Reflex — The Instant Grammar & Decision Engine Suite

Target Models: Viper (100M), Nano (~0.5M), Micro (5.0M), Mask (1.1M), Mini (7.1M)
Runtime: Pure-Rust AST candidate reflex engine & lightweight in-process execution.
Core Mechanism: Couples task-dedicated neural architectures with deterministic AST grammars, lexical intent matrices, and policy gates. By enforcing structural constraints and strict classification heads at the token level, the Reflex fleet emits verified syntax, terminal commands, redaction masks, and defect forecasts in sub-0.1ms to sub-20µs with < 28 MB peak resident RAM.

Pillar 2: v-µLM Vector — The Mathematical Codebook Lattice

Target Models: Skate (135M), Mako (150M) & Manta (494M)
Runtime: Vector Quantization (VQ) Codebook Engine with Importance Matrix calibration.
Core Mechanism: Rather than pruning tokens or forcing linear low-rank SVD decompositions that suffer from 30-layer compounding drift, v-µLM Vector quantizes weight tensors into non-linear vector codebooks (IQ3_XXS). This preserves >0.999 layer cosine similarity, retaining 100% of underlying foundation model reasoning and conversational Guru personas in under 83–92 MB on disk.
